# Verticals

The protocol is designed for five verticals from day one. The first reference use
case is aircraft-engine leasing because it has clear cash flows, maintenance
records, lease documentation and an established appraisal process.

## Aviation

Aircraft, engines, finance leases and fleet receivables. Evidence includes the
lease, appraisal, maintenance status, insurance and registration. Primary risks
are lessee credit, maintenance exposure, residual value and re-lease timing.

## Space

Satellites, orbital infrastructure, ground segments and service contracts.
Evidence includes capacity contracts, ground-station agreements, licensing and
insurance. Primary risks are launch delay, capacity utilisation, obsolescence and
regulatory approvals.

## Aerospace manufacturing

Tooling, components and inventory. Evidence includes purchase orders, inspection
records, quality certifications and inventory counts. Primary risks are
concentration, obsolescence, quality escapes and receivables.

## Drones

Fleets, operational capacity and logistics infrastructure. Evidence includes
airworthiness records, operator certification, route permissions and utilization.
Primary risks are regulation, airframe loss, utilisation and operator compliance.

## Cross-vertical marketplace

A secondary market for eligible tokenized positions. Listings are allowance-based,
settlement is atomic, and a platform fee is explicit. Transfer restrictions and
eligibility continue to apply on every fill.

## Adding a vertical

1. Add the category to the domain model and validation.
2. Define the evidence manifest and valuation methodology.
3. Add operational task types and oracle metrics.
4. Extend compliance requirements for the jurisdiction.
5. Add demo fixtures, digest checks and UI copy that says the data is synthetic.
6. Do not describe the structure as an offer until the legal work is complete.
