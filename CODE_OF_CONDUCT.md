# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, respectful and inclusive environment for everyone contributing to Aerospace RWA, regardless of background, identity, experience or affiliation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct, constructive and evidence-based.
- Respect differing legal, regulatory and technical perspectives.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Protect confidential information, personal data and security-sensitive details.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a financial or technical decision.
- Accept responsibility and apologize when mistakes occur.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Misrepresenting an asset, approval, audit, licence or financial return.
- Pressuring contributors to bypass compliance, security or review controls.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a regulated financial institution.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, request changes, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

This code applies in repository spaces and in public representation of the project.
